---
title: Dog bowl low-water local alarm
updated: 2026-09-29
---
Build a local USB-powered alarm that loudly nags the owner when a dog bowl drops below a chosen water level. Use a waterproof normally-open float switch, STM32F103C8T6 Blue Pill, transistor-switched 5V active buzzer, breadboard prototype, then a dry waterproof enclosure. No Wi-Fi or phone notification.
