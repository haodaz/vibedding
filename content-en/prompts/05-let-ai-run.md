---
title: Let the AI Run Commands, You Read the Results
summary: Can't type terminal commands? No problem. Learn these three sentences and you have a pair of hands that can.
---
## Three sentences
1. **"Run X for me, then tell me whether it succeeded or failed, and why."**
   Example: "Run tools/check-env.sh for me and tell me what's missing."
2. **"What did that command you just typed mean? Explain it one word per line."**
   Ask this once a day, and in a month you'll know it yourself.
3. **"Before you run any command that deletes, overwrites, or installs something, stop and tell me first."**
   Put this at the start of the conversation. This is where your peace of mind comes from.

## Why this works
Your strengths are **defining what you want and judging whether it's right**, not typing on a keyboard. The AI's strength is typing on a keyboard. That's the division of labor.

## When to type it yourself
- The high-frequency commands: environment checkup, flashing, serial monitor. Type these a few times yourself to build muscle memory
- Everything else, hand to the AI
