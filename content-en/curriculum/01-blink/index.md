# Module 1 · A Hundred Ways to Blink

Underneath everything in embedded is this: **a certain pin on the chip, at a certain moment, is either high or low.**

An LED and a button are the cheapest teaching tools for understanding that sentence. By the end of this module, "GPIO" will be muscle memory.
